\subsection{Channel-Aware Diffusion Evolutionary Search}
\label{sec:cades}

We propose CA-Diffusion-ES, an A*-free, gradient-free bi-objective trajectory optimizer for
non-differentiable three-dimensional radio maps. The method minimizes the 3-D flight distance
$J_{\mathrm{len}}$ and the layer-normalized HGNN-predicted channel cost $J_{\mathrm{ch}}$.
Building collision, tubular-corridor clearance, and inter-UAV safety are treated as constraints
rather than being mixed into the communication objective.

\paragraph{Offline trajectory prior.}
A conditional DDPM is trained on smooth trajectories generated by topology-constrained rejection
sampling in the urban airspace. The training corpus does not contain A* paths. Each trajectory is
represented by 30 three-dimensional control points, conditioned on its start and destination.

\paragraph{Diffusion initialization.}
At planning time, the start and destination condition the reverse diffusion process, which samples
the complete initial population from noise. No A* skeleton, A* corridor, or A* fallback is used.
If the complete diffusion population is infeasible, a small number of RRT-Connect topology seeds
is injected. These seeds do not query the radio map or optimize either objective; their feasibility
checks are counted in the black-box evaluation budget.

\paragraph{Channel-aware Pareto evaluation.}
For trajectory $q$, the objectives are
\begin{align}
J_{\mathrm{len}}(q) &= \sum_i \|q_{i+1}-q_i\|_2,\\
J_{\mathrm{ch}}(q) &= \frac{1}{|q|}\sum_i
\operatorname{NormPL}_{\mathrm{HGNN}}(q_i).
\end{align}
Only feasible non-dominated trajectories enter the external Pareto archive. The reported trajectory
is the archive member with minimum Euclidean distance to the normalized ideal point.

\paragraph{Truncated diffusion evolution.}
At every generation, constrained non-dominated sorting selects elite trajectories. Selected latent
trajectories undergo a short forward noising process and conditional reverse denoising. The truncation
step decreases during optimization, enabling broad exploration early and local refinement late.

\paragraph{Multi-UAV execution.}
Geometric paths are generated independently under the same static environment constraints. A common
time-window scheduler subsequently assigns start times and verifies pairwise tubular separation. Thus,
the current implementation is prioritized execution scheduling rather than joint multi-agent diffusion.

\begin{algorithm}[t]
\caption{CA-Diffusion-ES}
\label{alg:cades}
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\REQUIRE start $s$, goal $g$, HGNN radio map, urban geometry, trained DDPM
\STATE Sample an initial population from the conditional DDPM
\IF{the complete population is infeasible}
  \STATE Inject a small set of feasible RRT-Connect topology seeds
\ENDIF
\STATE Evaluate $(J_{\mathrm{len}},J_{\mathrm{ch}})$ and constraint violations
\STATE Initialize the feasible Pareto archive
\FOR{each remaining black-box evaluation batch}
  \STATE Select elites by constrained non-dominated sorting
  \STATE Apply truncated noising and conditional denoising
  \STATE Evaluate offspring and update the feasible Pareto archive
\ENDFOR
\STATE Select the normalized-ideal-point compromise trajectory
\RETURN feasible trajectory and Pareto archive
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}
